## 1. 02新增碰撞例：Y35是接触边界，不是正净空位置

文件 `docs/cnc-programming/02-machine-fundamentals/index.html`，Why It Matters新增段落。

原句说夹爪Y40、Ø10刀具，中心到Y35“without hitting the jaw”。若夹爪占Y≥40，且刀具与夹爪在X/Z方向重叠，中心Y35时刀具最外点正好Y40，是相切接触，净空0。还需说明靠近方向及高度重叠，否则这不是完整碰撞模型。

直接替换为：

> Assume the jaw occupies Y ≥ 40 mm and overlaps the cutter in X and Z. A Ø10 mm cutter has a 5 mm radius. At center Y35, its outer edge reaches Y40: this is tangency with zero clearance, not a safe stand-off. For an illustrative 1 mm geometric gap, keep the center at Y34 or less when approaching from smaller Y. Check the holder envelope and positioning uncertainty separately.

验收：间隙 `40 − (Ycenter+5)`；Y35→0，Y34→1mm。1mm只是计算例，不是未经核实的通用生产安全距离。
