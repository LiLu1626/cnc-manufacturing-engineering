Spindle and Feed Programming

The spindle speed (S) and feed rate (F) are the two cut-rate commands. Getting them right is the difference between a good finish and a broken tool.

What you will learn:
  • S command sets RPM; M03/M04/M05 start/stop direction
  • G94 = feed per minute (mm/min); G95 = feed per revolution (mm/rev)
  • On Haas lathes, G99 selects feed per revolution
  • Speed and feed come from tooling data, not guesswork

Spindle

S sets spindle RPM. M03 starts clockwise, M04 counterclockwise, M05 stops. Most milling and turning tools cut clockwise when viewed from the spindle.

Feed Modes

G94: F is feed per minute (mm/min). Used on mills. G95 (or G99 on Haas lathes): F is feed per revolution (mm/rev). Used on lathes and for threading. Check which mode your machine defaults to.

How to Choose Values

Look up the recommended cutting speed (m/min) and feed per tooth (mm/tooth) from your tooling supplier. Use the Cutting Speed Calculator and Feed Rate Calculator to convert to RPM and mm/min.

Common Mistakes

  • Using mm/min feed on a lathe defaulting to mm/rev — feed is 100x too high.
  • Starting spindle before the tool is clear of the part.
  • Running at a fixed RPM for all diameters — use CSS (G96) on lathes for varying diameters.

Practice

1. On a Haas lathe in G99, what does F0.2 mean?

Show answer

0.2 mm per revolution — not 0.2 mm/min. At 1000 RPM, actual feed = 200 mm/min.

2. Why does a lathe use G96 (CSS) for facing?

Show answer

As the tool moves across the face, the cutting diameter changes. Constant surface speed keeps the chip load consistent across the cut.
