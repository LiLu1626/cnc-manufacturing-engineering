Lesson 10 · Spindle & Feed

Spindle Speed and Feed Programming

Spindle Speed: S Command

S sets spindle RPM. Combined with M03 (forward) or M04 (reverse):

S2000 M03     (spindle forward at 2000 RPM)
S800 M04      (spindle reverse at 800 RPM)

G97 vs G96 (Lathes)

G97 — Constant RPMG96 — Constant Surface Speed
What it doesSpindle stays at fixed RPMSpindle speed adjusts automatically as diameter changes
Use forDrilling, threading, facing small diametersOD turning — keeps cutting speed constant
ExampleG97 S800 M03G96 S180 M03
Why it mattersSimple, predictableConsistent surface finish and tool life

G96 needs G50 to cap max RPM. When facing down to center, the diameter approaches zero — without a limit, RPM would go to infinity. Always pair G96 with a max RPM limit: G50 S2000 (max 2000 RPM). Learn more: Cutting Speed Calculator.

Feed Programming

ModeCodeUnitsUsed on
Feed per minuteG94 F300.mm/minMilling
Feed per revolutionG95 F0.2mm/revTurning

On a mill, F300 means the tool moves 300 mm every minute. On a lathe, F0.2 means the tool moves 0.2 mm per spindle revolution. Never mix these up — F0.2 in G94 mode would move at 0.2 mm/min (way too slow), and F300 in G95 would be 300 mm/rev (way too fast — crash!).
