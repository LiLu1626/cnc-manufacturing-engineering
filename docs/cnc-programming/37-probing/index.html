Probing Basics

A touch probe is a precision measuring tool in the spindle. G31 records the contact position. Work offset updates are your calculation, not automatic.

Level: AdvancedMachine: Mill
What you will learn:
  • G31 = skip-on-touch, record position
  • Probe variables hold X/Y/Z at touch
  • Work offset update is manual calculation
  • Probing cycles vs DIY G31

Concept

A touch probe touches the part and triggers a signal. G31 moves toward the part at feed; when the probe triggers, the controller records the current position into a variable. That position is a measurement — the controller does not automatically move the tool or update G54. You calculate the edge center and set the offset yourself (or run a canned probing cycle that does).

Why It Matters

A common mistake is thinking G31 automatically "sets work zero." It does not. G31 gives you a measured position; you calculate the edge or center and enter the work offset yourself (or use a canned probing cycle that does the calculation). It records — it does not move the tool or write G54.

How

Edge finding with G31

Probe approaches the left edge from the left at slow feed. On touch, #5061 records the ball center X. If the ball is Ø4 (radius 2) and touch X = -100, the left edge of the part is at -100 + 2 = -98 (the ball center is 2 mm to the left of the contact point). If the part is 40 mm wide and work zero is the center, the center is 20 mm to the right of the left edge: center = -98 + 20 = -78. You compute this and enter it into G54 X — G31 does not write it for you.

Common Mistakes

  • Thinking G31 auto-sets G54 — it records, you calculate.
  • Probing at high feed — probe is delicate.
  • Forgetting probe tip radius in the calculation.

Practice

1. G31 touches left edge at X=-100, probe tip Ø4. Edge position?

Show answer

-100 + 2 = -98. The ball center is at -100; the contact point (left edge) is 2 mm to the right of the ball center.

2. Does G31 update G54?

Show answer

No. It records the touch position to a variable. You calculate and enter the offset.
