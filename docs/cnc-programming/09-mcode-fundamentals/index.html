M-Codes and Machine Functions

G-codes control motion; M-codes control the machine itself.

Level: BeginnerMachine: Mill & Lathe
What you will learn:
  • Stop/end codes
  • Spindle direction
  • Coolant and tool change
  • Why M-codes vary

Concept

M-codes are hardware functions. Near-standard but machine-specific. Verify on your machine.

How

CodeFunction
M00Program stop (always)
M01Optional stop (switch ON)
M03Spindle CW
M05Spindle stop
M06Tool change
M08/M09Coolant on/off
M30End and rewind

Example

S2000 M03
G43 H01 Z50.
M08
... cut ...
M09 M05
G91 G28 Z0
M30

Common Mistakes

  • M00 vs M01 confusion.

Practice

1. M00 vs M01?

Show answer

M00 always stops; M01 only if switch ON.
