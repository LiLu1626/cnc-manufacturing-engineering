Linear Interpolation — Safe Approach and Cutting

Concept

G00 moves fast (no cut). G01 moves at feed rate (cutting). The skill is in the sequence: how to approach the part, plunge, cut, and retract without crashing.

The Safe Approach Sequence

Line-by-Line Tool Path

G00 X0 Y0         (1. Rapid to XY position, Z still at 50 = safe)
G00 Z5.           (2. Rapid down to 5mm above part)
G01 Z-2. F100.    (3. Feed down 2mm deep at 100 mm/min — PLUNGE)
G01 X100. F300.   (4. Cut along X to 100mm at 300 mm/min)
G00 Z50.          (5. Rapid retract to safe height)
G00 X0 Y0         (6. Rapid back to start)

Never G00 into the part. Step 2 goes to Z5 (just above the part), then step 3 switches to G01 for the plunge. If you wrote G00 Z-2., the tool would slam into the part at rapid speed. Always use G01 for any Z move that reaches material.
