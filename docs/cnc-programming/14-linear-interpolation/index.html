Rapid & Linear Motion

G00 and G01 are the workhorses of CNC motion. This page explains when to use which, safe approach patterns, and how to estimate cutting time.

Concept

G00 moves at rapid traverse (typically 15-30 m/min) for positioning only. G01 moves at programmed feed rate while cutting. Both are modal. The tool path between points depends on whether the move is rapid or feed: G00 may move axes independently (not straight line), while G01 always interpolates a straight line between start and end.

Why It Matters

The #1 crash cause is G00 XY motion while the tool is at cut depth. A safe program always: positions XY first, then lowers Z, cuts, then raises Z before the next XY move. Breaking this sequence crashes the tool into the vise or part.

How It Works

Safe Approach Pattern

  1. G00 to XY target (above stock)
  2. G00 Z to clearance (e.g., Z5)
  3. G01 Z to cut depth at feed
  4. G01 XY cut moves at feed
  5. G00 Z retract to clearance
  6. G00 to next XY

Cutting Time

Time = distance / feed rate. For a 50 mm cut at F200: 50/200 = 0.25 min = 15 seconds. This is steady-state time; add acceleration/deceleration for short moves.

Example

G00 X0 Y0        (position above hole 1)
G00 Z5.          (rapid to 5mm clearance)
G01 Z-5. F100.   (feed down 100mm/min)
G01 X50. F300.   (cut 50mm at 300mm/min = 10s)
G00 Z5.          (retract before XY)
G00 X100. Y0.    (rapid to next position)
G01 Z-5. F100.   (feed down again)

Common Mistakes

  • G00 XY at cut depth — always retract Z first.
  • Forgetting F on first G01 — the feed may default to a dangerous value.
  • Assuming G00 is straight-line — axes may move independently; the diagonal path may hit obstacles.

Practice

1. Cut length 75 mm at F250. Time?

Ans

75/250 = 0.3 min = 18 s.

2. Why retract Z before moving XY?

Ans

At cut depth, rapid XY would drag the tool through the part or fixture.

3. What is the difference between G00 and G01 path?

Ans

G01 interpolates straight line at feed. G00 moves each axis at max speed independently — the path may be two segments, not a diagonal.
