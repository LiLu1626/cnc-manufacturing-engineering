Cutter Compensation (G41 / G42)

Concept

When you mill a contour, you program the part outline — not the path the tool center must follow. The controller uses cutter compensation to offset the tool center by the tool radius automatically.

Why It Matters

Without compensation, you'd have to recalculate every coordinate by hand for every tool diameter. With compensation, you program the part dimensions directly. If the tool wears 0.1 mm smaller, you just update the offset — no program change.

G41 vs G42

CodeMeaningDirection
G41Cutter leftTool is to the left of the programmed direction of travel
G42Cutter rightTool is to the right of the programmed direction of travel
G40CancelTurn compensation off

Compensation must engage on a straight lead-in move. You cannot turn G41 on while cutting an arc. The standard pattern: approach on a straight line away from the part, engage G41, then cut the contour. At the end, straight lead-out, then G40. Engaging compensation mid-cut causes the tool to jump sideways — a crash.
