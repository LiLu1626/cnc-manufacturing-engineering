Roughing & Finishing Cycles

G71 and G70 automate the bulk of lathe work. This page explains the roughing cycle, the P-Q contour definition, and finish allowance.

Concept

G71 is the roughing cycle: it repeatedly cuts the stock down to the near-finished contour, leaving a finish allowance. G70 is the finishing cycle: it takes one clean pass along the P-Q contour at finishing speed and feed.

The P-Q blocks define the finished shape. G71 reads those blocks and automatically generates roughing passes.

Why It Matters

Writing roughing passes by hand for a 40 mm deep cut with 1 mm per pass means 40 passes — tedious and error-prone. G71 does it automatically. Getting the P-Q contour wrong (e.g., wrong X direction, or adding a groove) causes G71 to alarm or cut the wrong shape.

How It Works

Haas Single-Line G71

G71 P10 Q20 D1.0 U0.4 W0.1 F0.2
  • D1.0 = depth per pass (radial)
  • U0.4 = finish allowance on diameter
  • W0.1 = finish allowance on Z
  • P10 Q20 = contour block range

P-Q Contour Rules

  • First block must be X-only (no Z)
  • X must move toward the part monotonically (Type I)
  • No grooves or undercuts in the contour

Example

G71 P10 Q20 D1.0 U0.4 W0.1 F0.2
N10 G00 X30.
G01 Z0. F0.1
Z-20.
X36.
Z-40.
N20 X42.
G70 P10 Q20

Rough leaves O30.4 and O36.4 (U0.4 diameter). G70 at S1200 F0.10 cuts to O30 and O36.

Common Mistakes

  • Grooves in G71 contour — Type I G71 cannot handle grooves. Use G73 or manual.
  • First P block has Z — the first block after P must be X-only.
  • Forgetting G70 — G71 leaves stock; G70 is needed for final dimensions.

Practice

1. U0.4 on diameter. Radial finish allowance?

Ans

0.2 mm radial.

2. Why no grooves in G71 contour?

Ans

Type I G71 assumes monotonic X. Grooves require G73 (pattern repeating) or manual programming.

3. What does G70 do?

Ans

Finishing cycle: one clean pass along the P-Q contour at finish speed/feed.
