Turning Cycles (G70/G71)

G71 roughs a contour automatically with multiple Z passes; G70 finishes it in one clean pass. You define the rough stock, the final contour, and the depth per pass.

What you will learn:
  • G71 roughing cycle: automatic multiple passes
  • G70 finishing cycle: one pass at finish size
  • P/Q contour definition block
  • Monotonic X/Z requirement for G71

G71 Roughing

G71 takes a starting diameter and automatically removes stock in layers along Z until the contour near-net shape is reached. You define: depth of cut, stock allowance (X and Z), and the contour between P (start) and Q (end) labels. The controller generates all the roughing passes.

G70 Finishing

G70 runs the contour between P and Q one time at finish size, after G71 has rough-removed most of the stock.

Important Constraint

G71 comes in two forms on Haas/FANUC controls:

  • Type I (standard): Both X and Z must be monotonic within the P-Q contour — X moves consistently outward or inward, and Z does not reverse. Stepped shafts, tapers, and straight OD turning work here. A contour that backs up in X causes an alarm.
  • Type II (Haas/FANUC option): Z must still be monotonic (progress in one direction), but X may reverse — allowing simple recesses, grooves, and profile changes within the roughing contour. Type II is selected by the parameter setting or by specifying the P/Q blocks differently; confirm availability in your controller manual.

Always test with a dry run before committing. If Type II is not enabled, a contour that reverses X will alarm or cut incorrectly.

Common Mistakes

  • Defining a contour where X goes back and forth — G71 requires monotonic geometry.
  • Forgetting to leave stock for G70 — G71 leaves a finish allowance.
  • Not specifying the depth of cut — the cycle takes one cut.

Practice

1. What is the difference between G71 and G70?

Show answer

G71 roughs the contour in multiple passes leaving finish stock. G70 finishes the contour in one clean pass at final size.

2. Why must the G71 contour be monotonic?

Show answer

G71 assumes the tool moves consistently along Z while stepping in X. A contour that reverses direction confuses the automatic pass generation.
