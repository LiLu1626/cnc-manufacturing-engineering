G00, G01, G02, G03 — Motion Codes

Concept

G-codes tell the machine how to move. The four motion codes you'll use 90% of the time are: G00 (rapid), G01 (linear cut), G02 (clockwise arc), G03 (counterclockwise arc). This lesson teaches you not just what each code does, but how to use it safely.

Why It Matters

Everything a machine does is motion. If you understand these four codes deeply, you can read most of any program. The rest is just offsets, cycles, and housekeeping.

G00 — Rapid Positioning

Purpose: Move the tool at maximum speed to a position. No cutting happens.

Syntax: G00 X__ Y__ Z__

How it works: All specified axes move simultaneously at the machine's rapid rate (often 20–40 m/min). On many machines, the axes don't move in a straight line — they move at their own maximum speed, so the path may be diagonal or segmented.

G00 is dangerous. Because axes move at max speed and possibly not in a straight line, the tool can crash into the part or fixture. Never use G00 in Z near the material. Always: (1) rapid XY at a safe height, (2) then feed down in Z. The safe approach sequence is G00 XY → G00 Z high → G01 Z down.

G01 — Linear Interpolation

Purpose: Cut in a straight line at a controlled feed rate.

Syntax: G01 X__ Y__ Z__ F__

How it works: The controller coordinates all axes to move in a perfectly straight line at the programmed feed F. This is cutting motion.

Example: Cut a straight groove

G90 G54 G00 X0 Y0        (rapid to start position)
G43 H01 Z50. M08         (safe Z, coolant on)
G01 Z-2. F100.           (plunge 2mm deep at 100 mm/min)
G01 X100. F300.          (cut along X to 100mm at 300 mm/min)
G00 Z50. M09             (retract)
M30

Line-by-line:

  • G01 Z-2. F100. — Z moves down 2mm at 100 mm/min (plunge feed, slower)
  • G01 X100. F300. — X moves to 100mm at 300 mm/min (cutting feed, faster). Notice G01 and F are modal — they persist from the previous line.

G02 / G03 — Circular Interpolation

Purpose: Cut arcs and circles.

Syntax: G02/G03 X__ Y__ I__ J__ F__ or G02/G03 X__ Y__ R__ F__

CodeDirection
G02Clockwise arc
G03Counterclockwise arc

I and J are the arc center offset from the start point (not absolute coordinates):

  • I = distance from start X to center X
  • J = distance from start Y to center Y

Alternatively, use R (radius): G02 X240. Y160. R80.

Common mistake: I/J are incremental from start, not absolute. If start is at X80 and center is at X160, I=80 (the difference), not I=160. When in doubt, use R.

Practice Exercise

Question: Write the code to cut a straight line from (X0, Y0) to (X50, Y0) at 250 mm/min, starting from a safe Z height of 50.

Answer:

G00 X0 Y0
G01 Z-2. F100.
G01 X50. F250.
G00 Z50.
