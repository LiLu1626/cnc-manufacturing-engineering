Canned Cycles — How They Work

Concept

A canned cycle packages a repeated motion sequence (position → rapid down → feed down → retract) into a single line. Once activated, every X/Y coordinate becomes a new hole. This is how you drill a 40-hole plate in 40 lines instead of 400.

G98 vs G99 Retract

CodeRetract toUse when
G99R plane (e.g. Z5)No obstacles between holes — faster
G98Initial Z height (e.g. Z50)Fixtures or tall parts between holes

Cycle definitions vary by controller. G81 on Fanuc is standard, but some Haas or Siemens cycles have extra parameters. Always check your controller's manual. The structure (position → R plane → Z depth → retract) is universal.
