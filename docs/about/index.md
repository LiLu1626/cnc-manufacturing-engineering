# About

**Li Lu | CNC & Manufacturing Engineering** is a long-term technical platform for structured, practical, and professionally accountable manufacturing knowledge.

[← Platform Home](../)

## Mission

The platform connects CNC machining practice with engineering fundamentals, documented process learning, and responsible digital methods. Its purpose is to make technical knowledge easier to understand, verify, reuse, and improve across future projects.

## What This Platform Covers

The scope begins with CNC machining and extends where needed into the connected disciplines that determine manufacturing performance:

- CNC programming and macro programming
- Machine systems and machining process capability
- Cutting tool technology
- Workholding and fixture engineering
- Engineering tools and calculation methods
- Manufacturing knowledge and technical documentation
- Engineering case studies and lessons learned
- AI and digital methods for manufacturing

The architecture is intentionally expandable. New disciplines will be added as independent, professionally named areas when they require their own knowledge model, terminology, and validation approach.

## Content Principles

### Engineering Before Opinion

Content should explain the condition, assumption, limitation, and verification method behind a recommendation. Practical experience matters, but it should be expressed with enough context that another engineer can judge whether it applies.

### Clarity and Traceability

Articles, tools, case studies, and future resources should use clear titles, consistent terminology, stable URLs, and version-controlled publication. Where a method depends on a specific controller, material, machine, standard, or production condition, that boundary should be stated.

### Practical Usefulness

The platform is designed for real manufacturing decisions: preparing work, evaluating a process, investigating a problem, improving capability, and recording knowledge that should not be lost between projects.

### Continuous Improvement

Published material is not treated as permanently complete. Content may be corrected, expanded, restructured, or archived as new evidence, process experience, standards, or technology become available.

## Professional Boundaries

This platform provides educational and engineering-reference material. It does not replace approved drawings, customer specifications, machine-builder instructions, cutting-tool supplier guidance, site safety rules, formal training, or qualified engineering review.

Any recommendation affecting personnel safety, machine protection, product conformity, or released production must be independently evaluated and approved within the applicable organization and process-control system.

## Confidentiality and Respect for Intellectual Property

Public material must protect customer, employer, supplier, and collaborator information. Controlled drawings, proprietary programs, pricing, confidential process data, and identifying production details should not be published without appropriate authorization. Useful lessons can often be shared through anonymized examples and method-focused explanations.

## Platform Evolution

The current site is a foundation, not a fixed endpoint. Future additions may include metrology and inspection, automation and robotics, process development, materials and metallurgy, manufacturing standards, digital manufacturing, and other relevant engineering domains.

The governing principle is simple: expand deliberately, maintain professional structure, and preserve the traceability of useful engineering knowledge.
