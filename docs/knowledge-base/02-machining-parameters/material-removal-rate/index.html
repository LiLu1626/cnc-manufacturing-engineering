02.5 · Machining Parameters

Material Removal Rate (MRR)

MRR is the volume of material removed per minute. It is the direct measure of productivity — higher MRR = more parts per hour. Every cutting parameter choice should be judged against its effect on MRR.

The Formulas

Milling: Q = Vf × ap × ae   (cm³/min)
Turning: Q = Vf × ap × f   (cm³/min)
Drilling: Q = (π × D² / 4) × Vf   (cm³/min)

Worked Example

Face milling a steel block: Vf = 600 mm/min, ap = 2 mm, ae = 40 mm. What is the MRR?

Q = 600 × 2 × 40 = 48,000 mm³/min = 48 cm³/min

For steel at kc=3,500 N/mm², this needs about P = kc × Q / 60,000 ≈ 2.8 kW of spindle power. Check: Power & Torque Estimator

MRR and Machine Power

There is a hard ceiling: the machine spindle can only deliver so much power. If MRR × specific cutting force exceeds spindle power, the spindle bogs down or stalls.

MachineTypical powerMax MRR (steel)
Small hobby mill1.5 kW~15 cm³/min
Vertical machining center7.5–15 kW~60–120 cm³/min
Large horizontal mill22–45 kW~180–350 cm³/min

Why this matters: you can set the fastest feed and deepest cut in the world, but if the spindle runs out of power, the tool rubs instead of cutting — and you destroy the insert. Always verify power before committing to a high-MRR program.
