# Knowledge Base

A curated engineering reference that connects fundamental principles with repeatable manufacturing practice.

[← Platform Home](../)

## Purpose

The Knowledge Base converts dispersed shop-floor experience, technical references, supplier information, drawings, standards, and process lessons into structured engineering knowledge. Its role is not to duplicate manuals; it is to make decisions, assumptions, limits, and verification methods easier to find and reuse.

## Content Standard

Every technical article should identify its scope, the relevant machine or control context, the assumptions made, and the operational limitation. Where a claim is controller-specific, material-specific, or process-specific, it must be labeled accordingly. Where a recommendation affects safety, quality, or equipment protection, the required verification step must be explicit.

## Knowledge Architecture

### Fundamentals

Core principles that remain useful across machines and projects: engineering drawings, tolerances, datums, material behavior, cutting mechanics, units, geometry, and process capability.

### Applied Practice

Practical methods for setup, programming, tooling, workholding, inspection, troubleshooting, and continuous improvement. Applied content should connect a decision to its expected effect on quality, productivity, cost, or risk.

### Decision Records and Lessons Learned

Short, reusable records that describe a problem, its technical context, the evaluated options, the selected countermeasure, the result, and conditions for reuse. This prevents valuable manufacturing knowledge from remaining only in informal memory.

## Content Lifecycle

- **Draft** — Capture the idea, source, and initial scope.
- **Technical review** — Check terminology, assumptions, and limitations.
- **Published reference** — Present an approved, reusable engineering explanation.
- **Revision** — Update when processes, controls, materials, standards, or evidence change.
- **Archived note** — Preserve historical context without treating it as current guidance.

## Searchability and Reuse

As the platform grows, content will be organized with consistent titles, professional terminology, tags, cross-links, and clear URLs. Each article should make it easy to navigate from a symptom or design requirement to the relevant machine, tooling, programming, workholding, and inspection considerations.

## Knowledge Integrity

The objective is useful, traceable, and revisable engineering knowledge. A concise, validated explanation is more valuable than a large collection of unqualified information.
