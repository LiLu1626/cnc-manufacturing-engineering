03.5 · Machining Operations

EDM (Electrical Discharge Machining)

EDM removes metal with controlled electrical sparks — no physical contact between tool and workpiece. It is used for hard materials, intricate internal geometries, and fine details that a milling cutter cannot reach.

Two Main Types

TypeHow it worksTypical use
Sinker EDMA graphite/copper electrode is sunk into the workpieceMold cavities, internal gear shapes
Wire EDMA thin brass wire cuts through the part like a bandsawExtrusion dies, punch pins, fine profiles

When to Use EDM

  • Material is too hard (HRC 60+) for carbide cutting tools.
  • Internal corners require a radius smaller than any end mill.
  • Thin or fragile parts that would deflect under cutting forces.
  • Deep, narrow slots that milling cannot reach.

Limitation: EDM only works on conductive materials. It is slow (mm³/min) and leaves a recast layer (white layer) on the surface that may need removal for highly stressed parts.
