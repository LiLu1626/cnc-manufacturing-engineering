Formula and meaning

MRR = ae × ap × Vf

When all dimensions are entered in millimetres and feed rate is millimetres per minute, the result is shown in mm³/min and cm³/min.

For beginners: MRR is a planning indicator, not a safe-cut recommendation. Material, cutter diameter, tool overhang, machine power, toolpath engagement, coolant, and workholding must also be checked before increasing cutting load.
