Tapping strategy

Formula and use

RPM = 1000 × Vc ÷ (π × D)

Tapping feed (mm/min) = pitch × RPM — the machine axis must be synchronized with spindle rotation in rigid tapping mode.

Minor diameter ≈ D − pitch × 1.08256 (60° metric thread fundamental triangle).

Tapping uses much lower cutting speeds than drilling. Start at the lower end of the material range and use tapping fluid. Blind holes require chip evacuation; through holes are more forgiving.

For beginners:

Always confirm your machine supports rigid tapping (G84 with synchronous feed) before using these values. On non-rigid tap holders, feed is governed by the tap itself — never override it. Peck tapping (G83-style) helps in deep blind holes. Never run dry; use sulfurized tapping oil or proper coolant.
