Power and Torque Estimator

Estimate cutting power and spindle torque from material removal rate, specific cutting energy, and spindle speed.

Formula and use

Power (kW) = MRR (cm³/min) × Specific cutting energy ÷ 60

Torque (N·m) = 9,549 × Power (kW) ÷ RPM

Specific cutting energy varies substantially by material, tool condition, and engagement. Use this as an early-stage estimate, then compare with machine and toolmaker data.

For beginners: This is cutting power, not the complete machine demand. Allow a conservative margin for spindle efficiency, acceleration, coolant, and uncertainty in real tool engagement.
