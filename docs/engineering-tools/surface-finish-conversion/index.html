Conversion relationships

  • 1 µin = 0.0254 µm, so Ra(µin) = Ra(µm) × 39.37
  • Rz ≈ 4 × Ra (empirical, for general machining; varies 3–5× by process)
  • RMS ≈ 1.11 × Ra (for Gaussian surfaces)

Note:

Rz conversions are approximate. Actual Rz/Ra ratios depend on the process, tool wear, and material. Always use the drawing-specified parameter (Ra, Rz, or RMS) — don't substitute without engineering approval. µin is still common on American drawings; µm (Ra) is the ISO standard.
