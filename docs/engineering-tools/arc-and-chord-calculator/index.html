Formula and use

Chord: C = 2R·sin(θ/2)

Arc length: L = R·θ (θ in radians)

Sagitta: h = R·(1 − cos(θ/2))

Enter any two known values and the calculator solves the other three. All lengths in mm, angles in degrees.

Common uses: checking arc milling programs, laying out curved brackets, setting up radius gauges, and converting between chord/arc/sagitta measurements on a part drawing.

Note:

Leave unknown fields blank. The tool needs exactly two known values. Results are rounded to 3 decimals for lengths and 2 decimals for angles. For combinations involving arc length + chord or arc length + sagitta, an iterative solver is used.
