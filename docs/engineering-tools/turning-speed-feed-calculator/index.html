Formula and use

Spindle speed: RPM = 1000 × Vc / (π × D)

Feed rate: Vf = RPM × f (mm/rev)

Metal removal rate: Q = Vf × ap × f / 1000 (cm³/min)

Cutting speed Vc is the surface speed of the tool relative to the workpiece. It depends on the material combination:

  • Steel (P): 150–300 m/min with carbide
  • Stainless (M): 100–200 m/min
  • Cast iron (K): 80–150 m/min
  • Aluminum (N): 300–1000 m/min
  • High-temp alloys (S): 30–80 m/min

Feed per revolution f affects surface finish and chip load. Finish passes use 0.05–0.15 mm/rev; roughing uses 0.2–0.5 mm/rev. Depth of cut ap for carbide is typically 1–4 mm per pass.

Note:

Diameter D should be the cutting diameter. For facing, use the current turning diameter (not the bar size). Always verify the machine's max RPM — small parts may exceed the spindle limit. Feed rate on the controller is usually entered in mm/min (mm/rev for G99). Check your machine's feed mode.
