11.1 · Machine Maintenance

Preventive Maintenance

A CNC machine that's maintained well lasts 20+ years. One that's abused develops leaks, poor accuracy, and unexpected downtime. This is a general maintenance framework — always follow your machine's OEM manual for exact intervals and procedures.

Maintenance Schedule

IntervalTasks
DailyClean chips, check coolant level, check air pressure, wipe ways
WeeklyClean coolant tank, check lubrication oil level, inspect chip conveyor
MonthlyCheck way wiper condition, inspect tool changer, examine belts
QuarterlyCheck spindle runout, inspect hydraulic oil, clean heat exchangers
AnnuallyReplace hydraulic oil, check ballscrew preload, level the machine, laser calibration check

Never run a machine low on lube oil. The ways and ballscrews rely on automatic lubrication. A blocked lube line or empty reservoir will score the ways in days. Check the lube reservoir every morning.
